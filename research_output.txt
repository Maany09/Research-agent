## Summary
Quantum physics is a branch of physics that deals with phenomena on a very small scale, such as molecules, atoms, electrons, protons, and photons. It focuses on the behavior of matter and energy at these scales, where classical physics theories do not apply. Quantum mechanics describes how particles behave in terms of probability distributions rather than exact positions. The field also includes quantum computing, which uses quantum bits (qubits) to perform operations that are impossible with traditional binary bits.
